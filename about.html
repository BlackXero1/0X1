About

BlackXero1

Malware analyst and reverse engineer. I study how modern threats are built, how they hide, and how to catch them.

Background

I take malicious software apart end to end, from first triage to recovered payloads, decoded C2 and detection content that defenders can deploy the same day.

My work centres on reverse engineering custom loaders and implants, unpacking multi-stage delivery chains, tracking threat actors across campaigns, and turning every finding into detections mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.

I also study attacker tradecraft (evasion, injection and Windows internals) so that the detections I write hold up against how real intrusions work.
