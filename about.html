About

BlackXero1

Senior malware analyst and reverse engineer. I break down malicious software and write about what I find.

Background

I specialize in the full malware analysis pipeline — from initial triage and static disassembly through to live detonation, C2 protocol dissection, and IOC extraction. Every write-up is grounded in hands-on analysis: real samples, real syscalls, real network traffic.

My core work covers static reverse engineering with IDA Pro and Ghidra, behavioral analysis with Procmon and Wireshark, unpacking and deobfuscation, and detection engineering — writing YARA signatures, Sigma rules, and KQL queries mapped to ATT&CK. On the offensive side I research EDR evasion, Windows internals, and low-level syscall techniques to sharpen detection coverage.
